About

Matteo Sarrocco.

I work in digital transformation applied to the construction and infrastructure sector. I focus on optimising and standardising processes, driving their automation and simplification. I have designed and developed end-to-end systems and procedures for information management.

At Rete Ferroviaria Italiana, within the Stations Department, I coordinate digital innovation initiatives through information management, developing processes and tools that support the Contracting Authority in improving the quality, traceability and efficiency of the design and delivery of investment projects.
